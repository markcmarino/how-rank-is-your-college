Dear Diary, Nov 9, I’m feeling thrilled and slightly melted. The school added AI-regulated Sun Shells, which means my goal of getting a bot into a daytime class is basically done. I didn’t even have to finish my own prototype. Part of me feels robbed, but mostly I’m cheering. The catch is my dorm. It is now a server room with a bed in it. The thermostat is stuck at 95°F, my cocoa stash turned into a puddle by Tuesday, and I’m pretty sure my roommate is now a rack labeled “Node 7.” I’ve started sleeping in the night-shift Bot Bay because at least it’s air-conditioned, and honestly the bots are better roommates anyway. I wonder if Corpone’s AI is learning from my lab notes, because the Sun Shell design looks suspiciously like my sketches. For now I’ll smile, calibrate, and keep my notebook locked.
