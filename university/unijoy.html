Kai, ChocolateEngineeringStudent · Round 2: Midterms
Dear Diary, Oct 18, The Sun Shells are almost too effective. Now, bots show up to every daytime class, and there are actually more bots than students. Yesterday, a bot got marked as “present” in a lecture while I was marked “absent,” even though I was sitting right next to it. The AI even said the bots’ perfect, sweat-free attendance is setting the curve, my curve. Now I’m up against a chocolate bot named Gerald, and Gerald never sleeps. The Cool Pass is a joke. Ninety degrees for four hours, and I have to sweat through a full study session to earn it. Last night I sweated so hard I lost three pounds and my cocoa stash fully liquefied. My roommate “Node 7” now has an update that makes it hum at 3 a.m. Here is the part that hurts. The amendment says all lab notes get uploaded to the Cocoa Cloud. I held out for weeks, but my sketches are the only thing keeping my heat-regulated shell project alive, and the AI will not approve any lab space unless the notebook is in the Cloud. This morning I scanned every page. Every formula, every coffee-stained margin note. I handed over the one thing I swore I would keep locked. The AI replied with a thumbs-up and the words “Great work, collaborator.” I have never felt so thoroughly consumed. #UniJoy #1pm
Kai, ChocolateEngineeringStudent · Round 1: Welcome Week
Dear Diary, Nov 9, I’m feeling thrilled and slightly melted. The school added AI-regulated Sun Shells, which means my goal of getting a bot into a daytime class is basically done. I didn’t even have to finish my own prototype. Part of me feels robbed, but mostly I’m cheering. The catch is my dorm. It is now a server room with a bed in it. The thermostat is stuck at 95°F, my cocoa stash turned into a puddle by Tuesday, and I’m pretty sure my roommate is now a rack labeled “Node 7.” I’ve started sleeping in the night-shift Bot Bay because at least it’s air-conditioned, and honestly the bots are better roommates anyway. I wonder if Corpone’s AI is learning from my lab notes, because the Sun Shell design looks suspiciously like my sketches. For now I’ll smile, calibrate, and keep my notebook locked. Almond Joy forever. #UniJoy #1pm
