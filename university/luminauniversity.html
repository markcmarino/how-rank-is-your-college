College Fair Pitch

Lumina University is a small liberal arts college on the coast of Maine and overlooking the ocean. The colors of the school are navy blue and white, and a sea lion acts as the school’s mascot. LuminaU specializes in the arts, social sciences, philosophy, and business. Class sizes at Lumina are small in order to give students more individual attention, and socratic seminar style teaching is utilized in most classes. College Characters

Carla, Professor
Carla is a tenured professor of philosophy at LuminaU and has been at the university long enough to see how drastically it has changed since the implementation of these new AI policies. She is unsure of how the new policies will shape the campus culture, although she is only more driven to connect with her philosophy students. She enjoys the strong sense of community that Lumina is known for, and has played a large role in cultivating this in the philosophy department.
Jessica, Student
Jessica is a sophomore studying Psychology with a minor in Spanish at Lumina University. This semester has been a long one for Jessica, as she is looking for internships outside Lumina to update her resume and improve her chances of landing a job straight out of undergrad. She's extremely organized, passionate about psychology, and loves studying human interaction.

Secret Diaries

Jessica, Student · Round 2: Midterms
A lot of professors have been leaving, and my classes are now being taught by bots. I feel extremely concerned, and I am being told by faculty that I need to train a bot because of the new policies being enforced. I don't know anything about coding, and I am here to study psychology, yet now I'm being forced to study coding and learn about AI I have to participate in; my grades depend on it. I'm forced to now train a bot and essentially focus on coding the bot properly. All I want to be able to do is write my own papers and express my thoughts, not rely on a bot to express what it thinks I would say based on one sentence. I hope my professors come back or that I at least get some autonomy back and I don't have to participate in this coding anymore.

Welcome to Lumina, Jessica! I'm glad to hear you share concerns over the usage of AI, and saddened that it will overshadow your college experience. I hope you take this as an opportunity to connect with the current professors , and hope to see you in one of my classes some day.
Carla, Professor · Round 2: Midterms
More than half the faculty are gone now. For the first time in years, I’m struggling to get students to sign up for my classes. Apparently, the AI led classes are much more “lenient” on coursework. I was sharing my concerns with another professor a few days ago, and a board member seemed to have overheard us. The next day, I received a notice saying an additional round of firing will be taking place and that board members will be sitting in on lectures to make decisions. I don’t just love my job, I need it. I had to hand over all of my research and notes as part of my case to stay. I’ve put everything into my research, and now…they can do whatever they want with it.
Jessice, Student · Round 2: Midterms
Today my professor started implementing AI into the classroom. It's weird because I am a psych major; I am interested in human interaction, not much about technology. I guess I'm excited to see how AI is impacting people's daily lives and if I can find any clear changes in how people interact and socialize over time. I also want to make sure that my class doesn’t become completely driven by AI and instead remains a tool for students, but I still hope to have a lot of student discussions, as I learn best that way
Carla, Professor · Round 1: Welcome Week
I’m worried about the new policies coming into effect. Given, I was frustrated by the other faculty that would use AI to create their lectures or make their exams, but I didn’t think they would just cut out the middle man. I’m worried what this will mean for the reputation of the school. Such a big draw is our sense of community - how can we do that if there aren’t people to connect with? I just hope this will encourage other professors to be more creative in their classes.

Policies

As a result of new policies, we have been seeing increased fear within both faculty and students about the loss of human connection, but an extreme increase in students' ability to train AI bots. Our only choice now to continue to improve our school is to give students the okay to fully submit AI-generated sources, with their only job being to input the thesis. We believe this will challenge students further and make them better at wording concisely and understanding their bots more, although we note it might seem like we are handing everything over to AI. We hope to prove this wrong and show it through the skills our students will likely develop.LUM-5, Amendment, Round 2: Midterms

As a result of new policies, we have been seeing increased fear within both faculty and students about the loss of human connection. Therefore, what we will be doing is implementing a new policy that will call on professors. Human professors will have to give two in-person lectures weekly to ensure there is a slow progression of AI so it doesn't overwhelm both faculty and students. We believe this will allow students the time to discuss with their professors, while also building their AI skills and finalizing their AI bots.LUM-4, Amendment, Round 2: Midterms

After having implemented AI into certain professor roles, we have received feedback surrounding student concerns over differences in education value between human and AI taught courses. In response, we are amending the original policy. We will be expanding the policy to all current human-lead courses with mid to low ratings. All other professors are now being heavily encouraged to implement AI into their courses as well. To quell student fears of missing out on professor’s experiences, we will be using previous and current professor’s research and past lectures to train the respective AI models for each class.LUM-3, Amendment, Round 2: Midterms
  • Day 9: Under the amended "After having implemented AI into certain professor roles, we have...", everything is the same, except now it is mandatory.
Lumina University has implemented a new policy: 80% AI Essays. Students will now be expected to train an AI to write like them in their tone and continue to edit their AI to the best of their abilities. Once they create their AI to the best of their abilities, they will then be allowed to simply provide their AI with outlines for any essay assignments. Lumina University believes this will give students the opportunity to excel in their ability to shape AI and use it to save time and reduce cognitive load. This will improve students' quality of life.LUM-2, Amendment, Round 2: Midterms
  • Day 8: AI Corpone has praised "Lumina University has implemented a new policy: 80% AI Essays" as "very on brand." Admissions is unsure whose brand.
Lumina University has implemented a new policy that replaces professors that have used AI to create their lectures, with AI professors instead. This policy will not affect professors who have generated their own personal material for lectures. Through this policy, the school saves money by decreasing the amount of faculty and while also providing a new form of education. AI professors will be able to provide 24/7 help to students and ensure all students are treated professionally and earnestly. Concession: Some professors may be incorrectly flagged as having used AI and may have difficulty defending their position at the university. Students also miss out on possible networking opportunities by having an AI professor.LUM-1, Policy, Round 1: Welcome Week
