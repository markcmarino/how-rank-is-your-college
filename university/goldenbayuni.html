College Fair Pitch

We are Golden Bay University, located in the heart of San Francisco, California. We specialize in Environmental Sciences, Philosophy, History, and Mathematics, with a focus on combining technology, sustainability, and the humanities. Our university prides itself on being internationally friendly and tech-forward, especially through our new partnership with AI Corpone, which provides students with free access to the latest AI technology. Our mascot is the Golden Owl, and our school colors are International Orange, Deep Blue, and Gold, representing the Golden Gate Bridge, Pacific Ocean, and Golden State. Our slogan is "Wisdom for the World, Innovation for the Earth."
