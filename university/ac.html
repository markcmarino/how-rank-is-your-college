College Fair Pitch

We are a proud institution in Pembrook Falls, New Hampshire, where every student is pre-med, including the ones who don’t know it yet. Freshmen learn to take a pulse before they learn where the dining hall is, and the bell tower chimes at random hours so everyone gets used to being on call. In winter, students sled between buildings on retired hospital gurneys. Looking for work-life balance? Our career center can refer you to a specialist; the current wait is about four years. Mascot: The Ravens. Motto: Primum Nocere

College Characters

Orlando, premedstudent
Orlando is a junior pre med student at AC and the most competitive student on campus. He maintains a 4.0 GPA, sits in the front row of every lecture, and treats every classmate as competition for medical school. Known for reporting anyone he suspects of cheating, skipping labs, or sharing exam answers, Orlando has made it his personal mission to ensure nobody gets ahead of him. He is obsessed with becoming a surgeon and believes he is intellectually superior to approximately 99% of the student population. His goal this year is to graduate at the top of his class, secure a spot at Harvard Medical School, and remind everyone that he is, in fact, the smartest person in the room.
Theo, student
Theo is the only economics major at AC, which the registrar still treats as a clerical error. Every semester he gets auto-enrolled in Gross Anatomy and every semester he files the same appeal. He wants to land a finance internship this year and graduate without ever touching a cadaver, though he has already been listed as an "emergency contact" on three of them.
