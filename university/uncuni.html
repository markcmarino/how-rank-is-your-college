Secret Diaries

Meg, Student · Round 2: Midterms
Dear Diary, My god (Pardon my French). UU has gotten even crazier since the last time. I guess I wasn’t the only one complaining about the new policy or being unable to travel to LA. Ever since the administration patched the Wealth Bot policy, checkout lines around campus have turned into Judge Judy. Yesterday I stood in line while my bot argued that the sushi I was trying to buy would improve my focus for my psychology midterm, only to find out the “argument fee” wiped out three whole hours of tuition money I had earned under the new sleep policy. Truth be told, this was my first time trying sushi, and I don’t even think it was worth it. What was I thinking? Meanwhile, my dream of taking a bus to LA to see the church of Scientology continues to disappoint. That is, until I read the rest of the amendment. For the small price of giving up my privacy and location, I can finally go! Text messages, social media, and 24/7 GPS location data… any stalker's dream. Maybe that's what AI is: a stalker trying to learn all your information, and we are all willingly giving it up. I think if I tried to explain any of this to someone in my hometown, they would have a stroke.
Meg, Student · Round 1: Welcome Week
Dear diary, I miss Kansas. I left my family farm to learn about this AI stuff that is taking the world by storm, but what is going on at this school?? First, the brochure lied: LA is about a 2-hour drive from Palm Springs, so I guess my lifelong dream of seeing the church of Scientology in person is over. I suppose I can buy a bus or train ticket, but how can I with this new policy they have implemented? Instead of actually understanding how AI works now that I have stable Wi-Fi, my days are spent being pocket-watched by an AI bot! I tried buying a bag of chips at the campus store earlier, but my student ID was declined until I wrote a 500-word essay justifying to the AI how snacking supports my early retirement. This is allegedly supposed to keep me “relaxed” and not so worried about the future, but I’ve never been more stressed trying to buy lunch. I might need to ask the stupid bot if I can afford a bus ticket back to Kansas. Not sure this was worth the school mascot reminding me of Toto.
Jim, student · Round 1: Welcome Week
Bro I thought this school was perfect. I’m 34. I’m tired. All I wanna do is lift, get my degree, and open a gym. Now I can sleep after leg day AND get money taken off my tuition? Hell yeah. But last night I dreamed about my future gym. Had my name on it and everything. Woke up and AI Corpone sent me a notification saying they own it now??? Then I saw an ad for MY gym opening down the street. Same equipment. Same protein bar. They even used my face. Now I gotta come up with a whole new business idea and somehow not think about it in my sleep. This is crazy man. I went to sleep to pay off my tuition and woke up competing with myself.

Policies

In response to recent midterm feedback regarding student “purchase-justification fatigue” at the campus store, UncUni is amending our AI wealth bot contract with the Auto-Debate Campus Purchase Patch, designed to work alongside the new “Earn While You Sleep” sleep tuition initiative. The Patch: Students no longer have to manually write stressful 500-word essays to buy snacks or campus goods. Instead, each student’s personal AI Bot will automatically generate and debate the financial justification against the Campus Store AI in real time at checkout, instantly clearing routine purchases. Concession: Since this was not part of the original agreement with AI Corpone, every argument the AI makes on a student’s behalf will incur a micro-processing fee. This will be directly drawn from their “Earn While You Dream” tuition credits and 401(k) balance. Furthermore, due to all the emails we’ve received from students trying to travel to Los Angeles, any off-campus transit tickets (not related to school assignments) are permanently frozen unless the student hands over full access to their personal messages, social media, and location data so the AI can verify the trip is strictly career- or retirement-enhancing.
