College Characters

Randy, student
Randy is a student studying... bio? math? We've asked, but he never answers, and he rarely shows up to class. What does he do outside of school? Who knows?
JaneRobbins, AssistantProfessor
Dr. Jane Robbins is new faculty in the Geology Department, and she studies distinct rock formations across Northern California; her specialty, her obsession, and, honestly, her whole personality at this point. This semester she's teaching GEOL 210: Geology of Northern California and running a lab called The Bedrock Lab, where the rocks are local, the field trips are muddy, and the coffee is **strong**. Jane is also newly single — a widow, technically, but she'll tell you the story herself before you even get to the syllabus. Her late husband Kurt died on his biannual "boys n' rocks" trip after going down an abandoned mineshaft. He really loved geology too. In his honor, Jane started this semester by collecting Kurt's favorite rocks. Her office is now completely filled with them. On the bright side, they are very shiny!
Heather, student
Heather is a junior studying geology with a minor in environmental sciences at the University of Northern California. Last spring, she went on a maymester in Scotland to study the Moine Thrust, a tectonic fault zone that formed approximately 430 million years ago. She is incredibly passionate about studying plate tectonics around the world, and wishes to study abroad to escape her boring university, despite it being one of the top universities in the entire world known for geology.
