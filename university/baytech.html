College Characters

Logan, student
Logan is a sophomore studying Marine Technology at BayTech who dreams of becoming one of the world's leading oceanic researchers. Extremely ambitious and competitive, he takes great pride in attending BayTech. Although he takes serious time on his academics, Logan is quite a people pleaser and a bit of a party guy. He loves exploring new areas and learning new stuff with his friends. Despite his academic intelligence, he can be somewhat gullible when it comes to things outside his studies. His goal this year is to maintain a high GPA, secure a research internship, and hopefully make a name for himself in the field of marine technology.

Secret Diaries

Logan, student · Round 1: Welcome Week
Things have gotten pretty exciting here!! The university just announced two new programs, Project Atlantis and Operation Perfect Tide. I honestly couldn't be happier!! With Project Atlantis, students, like me, can now use and operate underwater drones and explore new parts of the ocean no one has seen before. I might actually discover a new species! That would look amazing on my resume. However, I did notice something in the agreement about AI Corpone owning all our discoveries. I mean, that's a little weird, but at least I can still put them on my resume, right? On top of that, Operation Perfect Tide is supposed to help improve our grades and make BayTech one of the top universities in the country. Sounds like a win to me! Some of my friends are worried about the part where students with low grades get reclassified as research assistants, but honestly, how bad could that be? You still get to attend classes! Walter seems pretty excited about the changes too, although he's already warned me that my recent academic performance is approaching an "unacceptable level." I'm sure he's just being dramatic. Anyway, I have a party to go to tonight, and hopefully Walter doesn't find out about this one. Things are really looking up for BayTech!
Logan, student · Round 1: Welcome Week
This is a really amazing school. So immersive and lively. What makes this even more great is that they are now assigning everyone a marine animal companion. That is so cool! I mean, having an animal you can talk to is pretty amazing. I ended up getting assigned an Orca named Walter, and things were going pretty well until I found out he was also responsible for evaluating my academic performance. Yesterday, I skipped a class to hang out with some friends and Walter somehow knew! This morning, I got an email saying my scholarship eligibility was under review because Walter reported that I was "academically irresponsible." I tried explaining that I was just taking a break, but making these loud clicking noises through his AI translator, telling me to reconsider my priorities. I tried giving him fish to help improve our relationship but apparently that counts as a form of bribery. I really need to keep my GPA up this year, so hopefully Walter and I can work something out. I'm starting to think he takes college more seriously than I do.
