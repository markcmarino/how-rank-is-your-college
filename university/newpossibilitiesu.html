College Characters

Sofia, student
Sofia is an environmental innovation major who loves exploring the different parts of campus. She is always coming up with ideas for new inventions, although not all of them work as planned. She once tried to create a machine that could control the weather above campus, but accidentally caused it to rain for three days straight. Despite this, she never gives up and believes every failed experiment is just another possibility waiting to happen. She hopes to create something that will make life better for people and the environment.
Sofia, student
Dear Diary, I think I've finally reached my breaking point. I came to New Possibilities University because I wanted to create things that could help the environment, but now I can't even work on an idea without AI Corpone approving it first. I tried to continue working on my machine that turns ocean pollution into reusable materials, but AI rejected it because it supposedly wasn't profitable enough. I guess cleaning up the ocean doesn't make enough money for them. It's frustrating knowing that all the ideas I once had are now being controlled by a machine.
