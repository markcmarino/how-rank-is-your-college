College Fair Pitch

University of Antarctica is a prestigious institution located in Marie Byrd Land. We specialize in environmental studies and have a 2% acceptance rate, making our program very prestigious. What makes our university unique is that we are completely desolate: no technology, no roads leading in or out, and no outsiders. We train our students to become climate warriors, given that our mascot is a Yeti. Our colors are blue and white.

College Characters

Clarise, survivaltrainer
Clarise is one of the nature survival trainers on campus. She helps train students in basic combat, how to wield a weapon such as a crossbow (used only in desperate times), and skills that help them live alongside Antarctica's wildlife rather than trying to dominate it. Clarise grew up in bayous outside New Orleans, which instilled a love of nature at a young age. When she came across the opportunity to teach students at UOA, she couldn't pass it up (no matter how cold she'd be). She thought it was a step closer to understanding the secret scientific missions that went down in Antarctica. You see, Clarise has always been skeptical of governments, but having so many countries studying Antarctica didn't sit right with her. She never tells anyone about her secret plots; she only writes in her diary.
Janet, professor
Janet is a professor of Wilderness Survival and Defensive Field Skills. Her classes are filled with knowledge of how to read the weather, navigate dangerous encounters with animals, and build protective ice fortresses. Every student must pass a respect-the-wildlife exam before they're allowed to touch a weapon. Janet is well respected by her fellow faculty and feared by her students. However, she is always looking for new recruits to join her secret climate task force.
David, student
David is a freshmen student at UoA, he is enrolled in four classes for this semester: Ice Fishing 101, English 110, Antarctican History, and Algebra II. He is quiet and enjoys wearing the school uniform, as he finds it stylish. He generally keeps his head down and spends most of his time reading—though, for the most part, this is done simply to appear mysterious and interesting to other students. A big reason he decided to attend UoA was that he hated the hot weather of his hometown in Arizona and wanted to study somewhere ice cold.
