College Fair Pitch

We are the University of Antarctica, a prestigious institution located in Marie Byrd Land. We specialize in environmental studies and have a 2% acceptance rate, making our program very prestigious. What makes our university unique is that we are completely desolate: no technology, no roads leading in or out, and no outsiders. We train our students to become climate warriors, given that our mascot is a Yeti. Our colors are blue and white. Our team members are Sydney, Jake, Gabby, and Cal

College Characters

Clarise, survivaltrainer
Clarise is one of the nature survival trainers on campus. She helps train students in basic combat, how to wield a weapon such as a crossbow (used only in desperate times), and skills that help them live alongside Antarctica's wildlife rather than trying to dominate it. Clarise grew up in bayous outside New Orleans, which instilled a love of nature at a young age. When she came across the opportunity to teach students at UOA, she couldn't pass it up (no matter how cold she'd be). She thought it was a step closer to understanding the secret scientific missions that went down in Antarctica. You see, Clarise has always been skeptical of governments, but having so many countries studying Antarctica didn't sit right with her. She never tells anyone about her secret plots; she only writes in her diary.
