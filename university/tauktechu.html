College Fair Pitch

We are a proud, efficient institution where every lecture has been replaced by a slightly longer lecture, summarized. Located in Northern Minnesota, students canoe around campus in warm weather, and skate from building to building most of the time. Looking for community, conviviality, a sense of belonging? OK, we have some of that too, for an additional fee. Mascot: The Fighting Summaries Motto: Per Prompt Ad Astra

College Characters

Ellen, groundskeeper
Ellen is a master gardener and likes to say she is the “mom” of TTU’s playing surfaces. The heavy vest she has to wear these days has something to do with “learning her movements,” but administration hasn’t explained why.
Tanner, student
Tanner is from Ontario and likes to play videogame soccer. He calls himself a history major, even though TTU has canceled the history program. He likes to take classes from other majors that contain a bit of history and combines them in his own handwritten notebook.
Sanya, student
Sanya is a math whiz from the upper peninsula of Michigan. She is currently working her way through her freshman requirements: Glee Club, Baton Twirling, Kreative Kosplay Society, and Dorm Gossip 100. Despite being required to outsource all her math to TTUs Math Progresser, she still calculates in secret on a chalk board she hides with a bedspread.

Secret Diaries

Sanya, student · Round 1: Welcome Week
So I just had my first meeting for my civil engineering research job. The job listing describes the job location as being Appleseed Library but the meeting turned out to be just standing in front of the library. Instead of a supervisor, the meeting was with another workstudy student doing the same work. He was hired just before me. We tapped phones and he gave me the workstudy App that gives the assignments. I have to do all the work on my phone, too, so it can track it all. He said if I had any questions I should ask the MentorBot on the app. His MentorBot was set for Biochemistry so it isn’t much help to him. And he can’t get ahold of anybody to change it to civil engineering. Then he said that every Friday at 7:00 I have to meet with the MentorBot who will assess my work for the week and adjust my salary. So I say: “you mean raises?” And the other student says: “raises or lowers. Depends how you do.”
Tanner, student · Round 1: Welcome Week
I managed to learn the basics about the Scottish Enlightenment today – Hume, Smith, Hutton, Watt – and I almost got busted for it. Since now only robots are allowed to go to class, it’s dangerous, man! Mando and Blee figured out that if you can get a counter-trained bot to play foosball with you they can tell you stuff they learned in class while you’re playing and it just looks like trash talking to the AcadCams. It helps if you look angry while you’re learning. We did real trash talking when the sweeper bot came around; it almost caught us. It’s so wild that one person in Edinburgh just came up with the whole idea of scientific geology as we know it, including our understanding of the history of the earth! If your bot is not counter-trained all they tell you is how cute the other bots in class are and how cool the other bots’ special hardware is: booo-ring!
Ellen, groundskeeper · Round 1: Welcome Week
Everybody insisted that the AI-created maintenance plan for Field C, our competition soccer pitch, was, like, totally correct. So I did exactly what it said. Oh, I had plenty of questions, but there was nobody to ask since they “optimized” our department. It was a lot of fricking work, too, lots more digging than we usually do. But now, now, now it’s right before our first game of the season and now there are tons of Athletic Department high-ups around pointing fingers and making me look bad! And I’m saying “If you didn’t want a carpet of daffodils and irises, your bot shouldn’t have told me to plant daffodils and irises”

Policies

Inspired by the NFL's radio helmets, TTU has instituted optiono-mandatory bluetooth knit hats for faculty, so that the good folks , or rather the good folksy bots at AI Corpone can tell them what teaching play to use next.TTU-3, Policy, Round 1: Welcome Week

In response to shortening attention spans, TaukTechU has replaced lectures with summaries, delivered by AI-generated fruit people in shortform vertical videos. Likewise, we have replaced our student theater with recited synopses; our sportball games with box scores. Even the major life events of students (joy of acceptance, disordered drinking, imposter syndrome, existential doubt, graduation) are now compressed into a short camera roll (set to the song “Unwritten”). We believe this will make our graduates the most efficient learners on the planet.TTU-2, Policy, Round 1: Welcome Week
