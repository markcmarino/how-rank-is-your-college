College Fair Pitch

We are a proud, efficient institution where every lecture has been replaced by a slightly longer lecture, summarized. Located in Northern Minnesota, students canoe around campus in warm weather, and skate from building to building most of the time. Looking for community, conviviality, a sense of belonging? Policies

Herewith and furthermore an addendum to TTU's "only robots" policy. Henceforward, students will be required to accompany robots to classes. Students will carry a cooler full of ice, issued by the Wellbeing Office, and use the ice to cool down robots in danger of overheating. Students are still forbidden to listen or learn.TTU-4, Amendment, Round 2: Midterms

We believe this will make our graduates the most efficient learners on the planet.TTU-2, Policy, Round 1: Welcome Week
